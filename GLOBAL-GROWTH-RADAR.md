## Current strategic findings

- Pinterest currently reports 640M monthly active users globally and says 96% of top searches are unbranded, making discovery content particularly valuable.
- Japan has direct Pinterest Ads availability; Pinterest also lists South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Hong Kong and India among partner-managed advertising markets.
- Pinterest requires country-specific policy review and recommends local language/cultural relevance.
- Pinterest's current organic API requires boards/pins read/write scopes for full board and Pin creation. Trial access can create test content but it remains visible only to the creator until Standard access.
- Tailwind SmartSchedule is account-specific. The connected account `sinaloainspireddreams` currently has automatically generated slots in America/Mazatlan.
- Tailwind currently has an empty queued Pin calendar. A Nectar Engine Pin generation has been started and is processing.
- CrakRevenue publicly advertises 350+ dating offers and GEO-specific advertisers such as AsianSingles and ShagToday, plus global and LGBTQ+ inventory. Individual offer terms must be checked before promotion.
- CrakRevenue's public material identifies APAC dating GEOs including China, Taiwan, India, Thailand, Philippines, and Indonesia as potential dating markets, but actual offer acceptance must be checked per offer.
- Meta's Ad Library can be used to research active Facebook/Instagram advertising by country, advertiser, and keyword before committing spend.

## Operating rule

Do not treat the ecosystem as a single Pinterest project. Pinterest is one distribution engine. Nectra is the campaign intelligence layer. Tailwind is a scheduling/distribution layer. Cantina and other future properties are destination/experience layers. Affiliate networks are monetization inputs, not the identity of the business.
