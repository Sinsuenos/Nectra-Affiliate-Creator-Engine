# Nectar Engine Platform Compliance Matrix

Reference export of the nine-platform compliance data currently published on the live Compliance page. Risk levels and guidance below mirror the existing published matrix.

| Platform | Risk | Banned / Flagged Triggers | Safe Posting Approach | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X (Twitter) | LOW | Direct link shorteners in bio-only posts; multi-level marketing language; unmarked financial advice; adult content; gambling links | Use #ad or the built-in Sponsored label. Avoid link shorteners that obscure the destination. Keep financial and health claims factual and cite sources. | 3 to 5 posts/day is normal for active accounts. Avoid identical affiliate content more than once per 24 hours. |
| TikTok | MEDIUM | Branded content without the Branded Content toggle; before-and-after health claims; crypto or token promotions; adult themes; misleading weight-loss promises | Enable the Branded Content toggle. Avoid hardcoded weight-loss or income claims. Use the disclosure tag in every video that includes a product link. | 1 to 3 posts/day. Rotate storytime, POV, green-screen, and other formats. |
| Instagram | MEDIUM | Affiliate links without Paid Partnership tag; health outcome guarantees; before-and-after transformations; crypto promotions; tobacco or alcohol targeting under-25 | Use the Paid Partnership label. Put disclosures in the first caption line. Avoid result-specific health language such as "will cure" or "guaranteed to work." | Feed: 3 to 5/week. Stories: 5 to 10/day. Reels: 4 to 7/week. |
| Facebook | LOW | Misleading health claims; fake urgency such as "only 2 left"; crypto schemes; adult content; multi-level recruitment language; cloaked affiliate URLs in ads | Use the Branded Content tag. Use Facebook transparency tools in ads and avoid cloaked links. Avoid disease-specific health claims. | 1 to 2 page posts/day. Boosted posts: 1 to 2/week per offer. Group rules vary. |
| Reddit | HIGH | Self-promotion without community approval; affiliate links in top-level posts on most subs; karma-farming patterns; duplicate posts across subreddits; undisclosed brand affiliation | Lead with value and genuine discussion. Place links only where subreddit rules allow. Disclose affiliation when asked. Check each subreddit wiki. | 1 to 3 relevant posts/week maximum. Do not cross-post identical content within 24 hours. |
| Pinterest | LOW | Health misinformation; misleading before-and-after pins; adult content; affiliate links in pin descriptions without disclosure; deceptive product claims | Put FTC disclosure in the first line. Use high-quality original images. Link directly to the offer landing page rather than redirect chains. | 5 to 15 pins/day is standard for active accounts. Avoid repeating the same URL on one board. |
| Snapchat | MEDIUM | Explicit sexual content or references to sexual activity; sex work, pornography, or nudity; dating services emphasizing casual sexual encounters; sponsored lenses with suggestive content; undisclosed sponsored content; content targeting under-18 without age-gate | Age-gate all dating-vertical content 18+. Keep the dating angle emotional and lifestyle-focused (companionship, connection) rather than sexual. Use the Sponsored label. Avoid 'casual encounters' framing entirely. | More permissive than most platforms for legitimate dating offers if content stays non-explicit and age-gated. 3 to 5 Stories/day. Spotlight: 1 to 2/day. |
| *Source basis:* Snap Advertising Policies + Snap Commercial Content Policy (snap.com/ad-policies, snap.com/terms/commercial-content), 2026. Dating ads explicitly permitted if no sexual-activity references. Suggestive content must be age-gated 18+. More permissive for dating-vertical content than most platforms. | | | |
| Discord | MEDIUM | Explicit or implicit adult sexual content; compensated sexual acts or escort service references; intimate massage or sex work references; products shaped like intimate body parts; unsolicited DMs with affiliate links; bot-driven mass messaging | Safe within private, consent-based servers discussing dating/relationships in non-explicit terms. Avoid escort or compensated-sex framing. Only post in designated channels. Get admin approval. Do not use public Discord discovery features. | MEDIUM risk - enforcement is structurally contained to closed communities, not broadcast/discovery risk. No public profile or algorithmic feed. Server rules control everything. Designated promo channels: 1 post/24 to 48 hours. |
| *Source basis:* Discord Ads Policy (support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/25516720403223), ~March 2026. Discord has no public profile, no algorithmic feed, no viral mechanism. Servers are invite-only/opt-in. Risk is server-level ToS enforcement, not algorithmic suppression. | | | |
| Telegram | HIGH | Adult materials on the official Telegram Ads product; illegal pornographic content on publicly viewable channels; phishing or malware links; unsolicited DM blasts; crypto scam patterns; content that could trigger app-store policy crackdowns | Do NOT use Telegram's paid Ads product for adult-adjacent dating content. Organic channel promotion carries real but inconsistent risk - treat as HIGH, not a reliable long-term channel. Prefer opted-in channels with clear disclosure. | HIGH for official ads (adult content banned outright). MEDIUM-HIGH for organic promotion since enforcement is inconsistent. Not a safe long-term bet due to periodic app-store (Apple/Google) crackdowns. Channels: 1 to 3 posts/day. |
| *Source basis:* Telegram Ads Guide (ads.telegram.org policies, via umnico.com/blog/telegram-ads), Telegram ToS, 2026. Official ads platform explicitly disallows adult materials. Real gap between formal ad rules and actual organic enforcement. App-store pressure forces periodic crackdowns. | | | |

> **Important:** This is educational and strategic guidance, not a guarantee of compliance. Platform terms change without notice, and enforcement varies by region, account history, and content category. Verify current platform policies before publishing.
