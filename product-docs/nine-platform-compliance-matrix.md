## Platform-by-Platform Detail

### X (Twitter) — LOW RISK

| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Risk** | LOW |
| **Banned Triggers** | Direct link shorteners in bio-only posts, multi-level marketing language, unmarked financial advice, unlabeled adult content, gambling links |
| **Safer Approach** | Disclose paid partnerships using X's Paid Partnership label and include clear identifiable disclosure in the post itself. A standalone #ad is not sufficient on its own under FTC guidance. Avoid link shorteners that obscure the destination. Keep financial and health claims factual and cite sources. |
| **Notes** | X permits properly labeled adult content; the risk is unlabeled or misleading adult material. Spam detection triggers on mass identical posting, not a specific frequency threshold. |

### TikTok — MEDIUM RISK

| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Risk** | MEDIUM |
| **Banned Triggers** | Branded content without the Branded Content toggle, before-and-after health claims, crypto or token promotions, adult themes, misleading weight-loss promises |
| **Safer Approach** | Enable the Branded Content toggle for posts that qualify as branded content. Avoid hardcoded weight-loss or income claims. |
| **Notes** | TikTok enforces against repetitive content that triggers spam detection. The enforcement threshold is behavioral, not a specific post-count limit. |

### Instagram — MEDIUM RISK

| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Risk** | MEDIUM |
| **Banned Triggers** | Affiliate links without Paid Partnership tag, health outcome guarantees, before-and-after transformations, crypto promotions, tobacco or alcohol targeting under-25 |
| **Safer Approach** | Use the Paid Partnership label for all branded posts. Place disclaimers in the first line of captions rather than burying them. Avoid result-specific health language like "will cure" or "guaranteed to work." |
| **Notes** | Instagram's algorithm may reduce reach for accounts that post a high ratio of commercial content. Enforcement is behavioral, not a specific frequency threshold. |

### Facebook — MEDIUM RISK

| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Risk** | MEDIUM |
| **Banned Triggers** | Misleading health claims, fake urgency ("only 2 left"), crypto schemes, adult content, multi-level recruitment language, cloaked affiliate URLs in ads |
| **Safer Approach** | Use the Branded Content tag for all sponsored posts. In ads, use Meta's transparency tools and avoid cloaked links. Health offers should avoid disease-specific claims. |
| **Notes** | Treat Facebook as MEDIUM-risk for affiliate content. Ad review, disclosure requirements, deceptive-claims enforcement, and restricted vertical rules can all affect delivery. Check the current Meta policy before publishing. |

### Reddit — HIGH RISK

| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Risk** | HIGH |
| **Banned Triggers** | Self-promotion without community approval, affiliate links in top-level posts on most subs, karma-farming patterns, duplicate posts across subreddits, undisclosed brand affiliation |
| **Safer Approach** | Lead with value and genuine discussion. Place links only in comments when allowed by subreddit rules. Always disclose affiliation if asked. Check each subreddit wiki for self-promo policies before posting. |
| **Notes** | Reddit's spam rules concern repeated, unwanted mass engagement rather than a specific numeric threshold. Each subreddit sets its own self-promotion rules. Build karma through genuine participation before any affiliate activity. |

### Pinterest — LOW RISK

| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Risk** | LOW |
| **Banned Triggers** | Health misinformation, misleading before-and-after pins, adult content, affiliate links in pin descriptions without disclosure, deceptive product claims |

### Snapchat — MEDIUM RISK

| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Risk** | MEDIUM |
| **Banned Triggers** | Explicit sexual content or references to sexual activity, sex work / pornography / nudity, dating services emphasizing casual sexual encounters, sponsored lenses with suggestive content, undisclosed sponsored content in Stories or Spotlight, content targeting under-18 without age-gate |

## Key Compliance Themes Across Platforms

1. **Disclosure is non-negotiable.** Every platform that allows affiliate content requires some form of paid partnership or sponsorship disclosure. The format varies (tags, labels, inline text) but the requirement is universal.

2. **Health claims are the fastest ban trigger.** Guarantees, before/after comparisons, and disease-specific language will get flagged across TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest.

3. **Adult/dating verticals need platform-specific handling.** Reddit and Discord are the strictest. Snapchat and Telegram are more permissive for non-explicit content but require age-gating. X and Pinterest are fine with proper labeling.

4. **Behavioral enforcement, not numeric limits.** No platform publishes a specific "X posts per day" limit. Enforcement is pattern-based: repetitive identical content, mass posting, and karma-farming behavior are what trigger bans.
